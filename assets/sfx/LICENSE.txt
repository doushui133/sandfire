Sound files in this folder (assets/sfx)
=======================================

The files were trimmed, mixed to mono, resampled and normalised with tools/sfx-build.js.

g_*.wav and m_*.wav — gunshots and gun handling
  "Snake's Authentic Gun Sounds" and "Snake's SECOND Authentic Gun Sounds" by SnakeF8
  https://f8studios.itch.io/snakes-authentic-gun-sounds
  https://f8studios.itch.io/snakes-second-authentic-gun-sounds-pack
  The author releases the packs for free commercial use with no credit required and has stated they are
  public domain (CC0). Credited here with thanks.

mu_victory.wav — victory fanfare
  "Victory Fanfare Short" by cynicmusic (made for Pixelsphere, http://pixelsphere.org)
  https://opengameart.org/content/victory-fanfare-short
  Released under CC0 (public domain dedication). Trimmed, resampled to 22.05 kHz stereo and normalised with
  tools/music-build.js. Credited here with thanks.

  "WARLORD - Video Game Announcer" by VoiceBosch (www.voicebosch.com)

  https://opengameart.org/content/warlord-video-game-announcer
